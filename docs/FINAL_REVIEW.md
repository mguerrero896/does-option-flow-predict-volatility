# Documentary acceptance review

**Status: CURRENT.** This is a desk review of the public presentation and its
linked evidence, not a study with recruited readers. The two-minute orientation
and under-five-minute acceptance goals below are design targets, not measured
reading times. No comprehension rate, participant result or scientific experiment
is claimed. The answers identify what the documentation supports and what remains
unresolved; they do not certify a release or replace execution checks.

The reviewed reading route starts with the [overview](../README.md),
[current evidence](CURRENT.md), [evidence map](EVIDENCE_MAP.md) and
[FAQ](FAQ.md). The following perspectives are analytical roles, not quotations
from actual reviewers.

## Twelve-question localization check

Acceptance requires a new reader to locate these answers correctly in under five
minutes. The table establishes documentary locations, not successful human
completion. A timed exercise with readers unfamiliar with the repository remains
unperformed. Each answer can be inspected without running a new scientific model.

| Question | Expected answer | Evidence location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is the research question? | Whether option state improves intraday volatility forecasts and whether trade-derived flow adds information beyond option state. | [Overview](../README.md), [current evidence](CURRENT.md). |
| What is the main current historical result? | Linear RV15 B2 improves mean QLIKE relative to B1 by approximately 0.623%, with p = 0.0032 under the declared one-sided sequence. | [Evidence map](EVIDENCE_MAP.md), [saved statistics](../artifacts/rp4_v4_b4/primary_statistics.csv). |

| Does it establish profitable trading? | No. | [FAQ](FAQ.md), [current evidence](CURRENT.md). |
| Are 160,832 forecast origins independent observations? | No; they belong to 419 evaluated sessions, and inference explicitly treats session dependence. | [Evidence map](EVIDENCE_MAP.md), [FAQ](FAQ.md). |
