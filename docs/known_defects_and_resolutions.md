# Known defects and resolutions

This register follows documented failures to their producers, callers and regression checks. It covers the [methodology decisions](methodology_decisions.md), the [RP2 validation audit](rp2/VALIDATION_REPORT.md), the [RP2 final report](rp2/FINAL_REPORT.md), the [PIT v2.1](pit_v21_decision_ledger.md) and [v2.2](pit_v22_decision_ledger.md) ledgers, the Phase 8 remediation, and the published RP4 materialization and closeout audits. Repeated reports of the same mechanism are combined. A repaired implementation does not make its earlier outputs valid: the original measurements, withdrawals and seals retain their recorded status.

**Resolved** means that the current implementation contains the stated repair and the linked regression provides evidence for that mechanism. **Superseded** means that the historical implementation or interpretation is outside the registered v4 path; it does not mean its original bytes were repaired. **Open** identifies an unresolved defect, a missing verification, or an evidentiary limitation, with its scope stated explicitly. A limitation is not automatically a programming bug.

The public history starts at `0274634` (the initial research release). Many earlier repairs and their tests are already present in that commit. In the tables, **P0** means “repair present in public commit `0274634`”, not “the original repair was authored in that commit”. Later abbreviated commits were checked with local Git history. Historical repair hashes that cannot be resolved in the public ancestry are not invented or substituted silently. The verification record at the end separates checks executed for this publication refresh from historical receipts and unavailable checks.

## What the v4 result actually depends on

The [v4 evaluator](../artifacts/rp4_v4_code/evaluate_v4.py) calls the frozen v3 `fit_ridge` and v2 `fit_lightgbm`, with the v3 preprocessing/inference and the registered v4 horizon contract. Its [target materializer](../artifacts/rp4_v4_code/materialize_targets.py) uses the shared RP2 calendar/grid and realized-variance definitions. The v3 gamma materializer reuses the registered RP2 tape/clock functions through its causal adapter. Therefore the calendar, source cutoff, missing-bar, keyed-join and causal-prefix repairs matter to v4.

The v4 fitting path does **not** call RP2 `cross_fitted_residuals`, the partial-pooling diagnostic, or either Torch network. Those historical components remain inspectable; their limitations are not evidence that the frozen v4 ridge or LightGBM result is invalid. Conversely, tests on synthetic inputs do not establish provider receipt-time semantics, remove historical selection bias, or produce a new scientific result.

## Interpretation and evidence gaps retained deliberately

The aggregate audit counts in decision 114 (40 findings, then 19, then 17) are counts of successive review passes, not an independently itemized set of 76 distinct code defects. The mechanisms named in that decision are included above. No missing individual finding record is reconstructed from those totals. The [historical risk register](risk_register.md) retains the other risks—licensing, prior exposure, campaign multiplicity, calibration, backup custody, and literature access—as risks rather than fictional bugs fixed by a test.

The following boundaries remain material:

- **B1 selection and PIT:** D15 and D58 remain empirical evidence limitations. A passing parser/cutoff regression does not provide an independent quote census or a historical client receipt timestamp.
- **Historical implementation recovery:** D51 and D79 remain incomplete historical reproduction/pointer evidence. Frozen artifacts are not edited to make a check green.
- **Registered execution portability:** D83 remains open. The public metadata check is a separate documentary verification path; executing the frozen chain still requires its original registered environment, paths and custody.
- **RP2 DML:** D53 is a real remaining guard-order defect outside the v4 path. The caller-width tripwire is a mitigation, not the shared-function repair.
- **Neural and GPU reproducibility:** D41–D46 contain historical fixes whose current optional runtime was not installed or executed. No GPU determinism result is claimed from CPU/public contract checks.
- **Secondary inference:** D72 remains a numerically reproducible but unreliable MZ secondary. D76 preserves the absence of RV30 bitmask proof. Reusing the same historical windows across corrected versions is not independent replication or a multiplicity reset.
- **Scope of success:** `RESEARCH_ONLY`, `NOT INVESTMENT ADVICE`, and `capital_go=false` remain binding. The v4 headline and the separately authorized exploratory v5 extension must be read under their own version/registration boundaries; this defect register does not promote either into a trading deployment.
