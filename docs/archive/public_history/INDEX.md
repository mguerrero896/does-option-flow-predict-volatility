# Historical research documents

These English summaries explain preserved specifications, stage reports, numerical audits and earlier publication records. The historical reading copies retain their original language. They document earlier states and do not replace the current results or authorize repeating consumed evaluations.

Private routing information may be removed in a public projection under a separate redaction receipt. That receipt retains the original provenance hash and records a different hash for the public copy; it does not claim byte equality. Scientific numbers remain subject to independent checks.

Some internal operational sources are not distributed. Their separate metadata records verify only a hashed path identity, historical content pin and current absence. Such a record supplies no executable content or numerical value; all sources selected for scientific claims must remain readable and independently checked.
