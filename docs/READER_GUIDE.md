## Editorial rules

1. Answer the research question before introducing version names or operational
   history. State the population, target and historical status on the first screen.
2. Put adverse evidence next to favorable estimates. Do not imply that a mixed
   feature-block contrast identifies informed trading or dealer hedging.
3. Keep the short reading route distinct from the full chronological record.
   Preserve historical measurements and corrections, but do not require them on
   the front page merely to satisfy a text-matching test.
4. Link each quantitative claim to an identifiable saved row. Keep the sample,
   denominator, uncertainty and testing gate visible. Do not combine historical
   windows or independent studies into a single claim.
5. Distinguish public aggregate verification, licensed reconstruction and new
   prospective evidence. A passing contract or a hash is not an independent refit.
6. Use relative links, descriptive link text and image alternatives. Essential
   conclusions also appear as text and tables, so the cover is never required to
   understand the evidence. Keep chart units separate from trading returns.

These choices apply GitHub's recommendations on purpose, getting started and
relative navigation, with detailed material off the entry page.
[GitHub README guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/about-readmes).
The explicit data-access and metadata boundary follows the distinction between
open and restricted research objects described in
[The Turing Way: research data management](https://book.the-turing-way.org/reproducible-research/rdm/).

## Visual provenance

The current motion overview has a [transcript, source and rendering procedure](figures/research_motion/README.md).
It replaces the schematic surface with a chart reconstructed from public session
loss aggregates. Generated particles remain decoration; labels and curves are
rendered separately from the generative background.

The earlier [static cover](figures/public_refresh/research_cover.png) is an AI-assisted editorial
illustration made with the built-in image-generation tool, not an empirical plot
or a model output. Company marks identify the six stocks; no affiliation or
endorsement is implied. SPY and QQQ are labeled separately as index-tracking ETFs
in the extension. The wireframe surface is schematic, with no empirical values.

Generation specification: a sober landscape scientific cover in navy, ivory,
teal and amber; title “Options Order Flow & Intraday Volatility”; subtitle
“Historical forecasting evidence under source-time assumptions”; six stock
marks and tickers; separate SPY/QQQ extension; option-contract identity fields;
three nested information sets; 15-minute variance forecast; historical,
non-confirmatory and non-trading boundary. The revision separates stock-bar
inputs to B0 from option-record inputs to B1/B2. No licensed image or data inputs
were supplied. The illustration is not reproducible byte-for-byte from a prompt.

The nested-set SVG is generated from the existing
[figure producer](figures/public_refresh/render_readme_figures.py), not the image
generator. Its counts remain 29/69/138. The method flowchart is native Mermaid.
Numeric result charts in the walkthrough use saved public aggregates and retain
their [source hashes and regeneration procedure](figures/public_refresh/README.md).

Option terminology is educational, not a proposed trade. The contract diagram
separates underlying, call/put, strike and expiry, consistent with the
[Options Industry Council introduction](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/options-basics).

## Public versus local

The public route is RP4 v4 and its explicitly labeled historical extensions.
Separate local HARQ/K3 work does not replace that result by virtue of being newer.
No private paths, raw observations, sealed cohorts or operational instructions
are needed for the public reading route. The presence of additional local work
does not establish that it is eligible for publication or independent evidence.
