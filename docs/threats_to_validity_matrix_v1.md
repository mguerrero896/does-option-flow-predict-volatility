# Threats to validity (current presentation: RP4 v4)

**Status: CURRENT.** Maintained validity assessment. See the [current research summary](CURRENT.md) for the active result and its evidence limits.

This matrix follows [canonical state](../data/CANONICAL_STATE.json). RP4 v4 has a
small conditional linear forecasting improvement; independent global confirmation
and economic alpha remain unestablished. Earlier RP2, PIT and Phase 8 evidence
retains its own status in the [findings ledger](scientific_findings_ledger.md).

| # | Threat | Evidence record | Mitigation | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adaptive campaigns and multiplicity | V4 is the fourth evaluation of reused windows. The v5 extension is exploratory. | Preserve each registration, correction and null; within-family H1→H2 controls only its declared sequence. | Cross-version search is not adjusted; historical success is not independent replication. |
| 2 | Model-family dependence | Linear RV15 B2/B1 improves +0.623%; trees −0.115% with p = 0.6280. | Report both families on the same eligible sample and keep primary mean tests distinct from medians. | No universal B1/B2 claim is supported. |
| 3 | Calibration versus information | QLIKE rewards calibration; results changed after producer repairs. | Report stored mean loss, MAE/RMSE and calibration diagnostics in their registered roles. | A loss improvement does not identify informed trading, dealer hedging or economic value. |
| 4 | Model and horizon search | V1–v4 changed coverage, numerical treatment and target horizon. | Record all versions, conditional predeclaration and the shorter-horizon estimand. | Differences across versions do not isolate a repair's causal effect or prove the optimal horizon. |
| 5 | Serial dependence and interval calibration | Overlapping intraday origins; 419 primary sessions and 25 final-window sessions. | Whole-session paired inference, 9,999 circular resamples in five-session blocks; 60-minute purge/embargo. | No full-sequence confirmation in the final window. The historical RP2 0.784 coverage diagnostic is not a v4 calibration estimate. |
| 6 | Target choice | RP2 compared RV60/RV30; v3 used RV30. | Decision closed: v4 has 15-minute primary and 5-minute secondary horizons; RV60 is historical. | Horizon preference is sample- and design-dependent. |
| 7 | Point-in-time availability | Source timestamps exist; historical client receipt is unavailable. | Six-asset forecasts use a 120-second source-time proxy. The data gap remains explicit and unfilled. | Source-time PIT does not demonstrate real-time client availability. |
| 8 | Licensed-data reproducibility | Raw bars, option tape and granular panels cannot be redistributed. | Public aggregates, hashes, schemas and code; private bucket with managed access. | Full feature reconstruction and model refits require entitlements and exact input hashes. |
| 9 | Regime and asset stability | RV15 linear flow is positive in six of six assets; RV5 in three of six. | Keep horizon and family attached to every stability claim. | Six correlated assets are not six independent replications; transport to new regimes is unproven. |
| 10 | Microstructure measurement | Realized variance and option-flow predictors are provider-derived measurements. | State bar labels, source clocks, regular-session rules and missingness exclusions. | Different feeds, quote conventions and microstructure noise may change results. |
| 11 | Common-complete selection | V4 primary sample has 160,832 eligible origins. | Compare B0/B1/B2 on a common valid mask; no unregistered imputation. | The result applies to retained origins, not every market interval. The PIT successor's 62,254 origins belong to another study. |
| 12 | Narrow universe | AAPL, AMZN, META, MSFT, NVDA and TSLA. | Identify outcome assets; SPY/QQQ are controls. | No broad-market or broader cross-sectional generalization. |
| 13 | Causal and economic interpretation | Earlier DML did not replicate uniformly; RP2 economic rules failed. | Observational and predictive labels; no conversion of QLIKE reduction into profit. | Confounding, proxy error, execution costs and untested mechanisms remain. |
| 14 | Prospective power and timing | Twenty new sessions, forty-session stability, secondary 25+20 combination and a 335-session extension. RP3 has its own gate. | Preserve registered roles and report the 335-session plan as marginal H2 normal-approximation power. | No guaranteed success, joint 80% power or completed prospective result is claimed. Phase 8 was already consumed and cannot supply new confirmation. |
